# Policy Catalog

This catalog lists approved governance and operating policies for AIArbiTech YouTube OS.

## Policy List

| Policy Name | Short Description | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Creator Policy | Defines creator-facing usage principles, onboarding expectations, and platform behavior boundaries for creator workflows. | Creator, Platform |
| Monetization Policy | Governs monetization eligibility, monetization controls, and policy-constrained monetization behavior inside YouTube OS. | Creator, Admin, Platform |
| Revenue Sharing Policy | Defines approved revenue-sharing principles and payout-related policy constraints, including auditability requirements. | Creator, Admin, Platform |
| Subscription Policy | Defines subscription-related rules, entitlement dependency, and policy alignment with Global billing/subscription authority. | Creator, Admin, Platform |
| Content Policy | Defines content quality, compliance, copyright safety, and publication readiness requirements before release. | Creator, AI, Admin, Platform |
| President Authority | Defines President panel as strategic authority for oversight, direction, and delegated execution. | President, Platform |
| Heir Authority | Defines Heir authority boundaries as high-level governance role with explicit financial operation restrictions. | President, Platform |
| Admin Authority | Defines Admin operational authority for exception handling, execution support, and governed operational controls. | Admin, System, Platform |
| AI Decision Policy | Defines AI decision boundaries, approval constraints, audit requirements, and prohibition of self-escalated AI authority. | AI, System, Security, Platform |
| Security Policy | Defines security-first controls, least privilege, zero trust, explicit allow rules, and mandatory audit for critical operations. | Security, System, Admin, AI, Platform |
| Platform Independence | Establishes repository, deployment, database, and business-logic independence from other platforms. | Platform, System |
| Architecture Protection | Protects approved architecture boundaries and prohibits unauthorized coupling, duplication, and prohibited dependencies. | Platform, System, Security |
| Change Management | Requires Architecture Review, approval traceability, authorized sprint execution, testing, and audit before controlled change. | Platform, System, Admin, President |

## Notes

- Scope labels are normalized to: `Creator`, `Admin`, `President`, `AI`, `System`, `Security`, `Platform`.
- This catalog is documentation and governance alignment artifact. It does not grant runtime permissions by itself.
