# Creator Handbook

This handbook explains how a Creator uses YouTube OS in daily operations.

## 1. Onboarding (How a Creator Registers)

Creator onboarding follows a guided and policy-controlled flow:

1. Open the Creator entry point and start onboarding.
2. Complete required profile details and platform preferences.
3. Verify account access through approved identity boundary.
4. Accept platform policies and operating terms.
5. Finish onboarding checklist and activate Creator workspace.

Onboarding notes:

- Access is permission-based and auditable.
- Missing verification or policy acceptance keeps onboarding incomplete.

## 2. Channel Creation and Management

After onboarding, Creator can manage channels in a governed flow:

1. Open Channel section and select Add/Link channel.
2. Complete channel verification and ownership checks.
3. Set channel metadata and operating preferences.
4. Monitor channel health, growth, and compliance indicators.
5. Update settings when strategy or policy requires changes.

Creator can manage:

- Channel profile settings.
- Content planning scope per channel.
- Operational preferences inside approved limits.

Creator cannot:

- Bypass security/ownership checks.
- Access other creators' private channel data.

## 3. Content (Video) Creation Process

Content creation runs through structured workflow stages:

1. Idea and planning
   Creator defines topic, goals, target audience, and release intent.
2. Draft and production
   Creator prepares script/content assets with AI assistance if needed.
3. Quality and policy checks
   Content is validated for quality, compliance, and copyright safety.
4. Approval and scheduling
   Approved content enters publish workflow with planned schedule.
5. Publication and tracking
   Performance metrics are tracked after release.

Best practice:

- Treat policy/compliance checks as required gates, not optional steps.

## 4. Monetization and Subscription Model

Monetization and subscription behavior is policy-governed:

- Subscription determines creator access to monetization capabilities and related features.
- Monetization recommendations are shown based on eligibility, performance, and policy constraints.
- Financial infrastructure is centralized and consumed through approved boundaries.

Creator actions:

- Review monetization eligibility status.
- Enable approved monetization options where available.
- Follow recommendations to improve subscription-linked outcomes.

## 5. Where Revenue Is Visible

Revenue visibility is provided in creator-facing reporting views:

- Revenue summary cards (current period and trend).
- Channel-level performance and monetization contribution.
- Subscription-linked revenue indicators.
- Historical comparison views for growth analysis.

Important:

- Revenue views are reporting surfaces; direct financial transfer controls are not managed by Creator.

## 6. Using AI Assistant and Workflows

Creator can use AI Assistant and workflows to speed up daily work:

- AI Assistant helps with planning, optimization suggestions, and content guidance.
- Workflow views show task progress, blocked steps, and required approvals.
- Creator can trigger approved actions and provide required inputs for next steps.

Recommended usage pattern:

1. Start with a clear objective (content goal, growth target, or optimization need).
2. Use AI suggestions to prepare actions.
3. Execute through workflow steps.
4. Review outcomes and iterate.

## Notes

- Creator operations must remain within policy, security, and governance boundaries.
- Critical operations are logged for audit and service quality.
