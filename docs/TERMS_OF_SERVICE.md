- Users must follow platform policy, security, and legal requirements.

- Access is permission-based and may be modified for security reasons.
- Critical operations are logged and auditable.
- Security incidents may require temporary service restrictions.

## Data and Privacy

- Data handling follows approved privacy and retention policies.
- Cross-platform data movement is contract-governed.

## Service Availability and Changes

- Service availability is managed by operational runbooks and incident policy.
- Changes to significant service behavior require approved governance process.

- Violations may result in warning, temporary suspension, or access removal.
- Severe or repeated violations are escalated through governance and security channels.
