# Creator Agreement

## Purpose

This agreement defines Creator responsibilities, platform commitments, and operating boundaries in YouTube OS.

## Creator Responsibilities

- Provide accurate account and channel information.
- Follow content, copyright, and compliance rules.
- Use platform automation only within approved policies.
- Report critical issues affecting account or channel safety.

## Platform Commitments

- Provide creator-facing operational visibility and support workflows.
- Protect creator data using policy-governed security controls.
- Maintain auditability for sensitive actions impacting creators.

## Content and Compliance

- Creator remains responsible for lawful and policy-compliant content.
- Platform may require review checkpoints before publication actions.
- Non-compliant behavior may trigger restrictions or escalation.

## Monetization and Revenue Context

- Monetization and subscription behavior follows approved policy constraints.
- Financial infrastructure is governed by Global shared services boundaries.
- Revenue views are informational and controlled by approved reporting flows.

## AI and Workflow Usage

- AI guidance is assistive and policy-bounded, not unrestricted authority.
- High-impact actions may require manual approval and audit trace.

## Enforcement and Escalation

- Violations are handled through Admin and security governance process.
- Severe incidents may result in temporary suspension while review is completed.
